## Purpose

This directory records a no-network, documentation-only architecture preflight for a hands-first spatial operations surface derived from Nymrel Swarm Studio. It is preparation for a possible Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 entry and a reusable Nymrel operator-deck capability. It is not an entry, build, deployment, legal acceptance, or eligibility claim.

## Product decision

Nymrel's provisional product direction is **Spatial Ops Room**, a seated, hands-first view of synthetic worker jobs, evidence receipts, approval gates, and incident timelines.

Because Nymrel Swarm Studio existed before the competition window, the conservative proposed division is **Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience**. The planned WebXR spatial interaction surface must be a meaningful new user-facing capability; Meta retains discretion over whether it qualifies.

Provisional targets:

- Productivity track.
- Best Agentic Interaction special award.
- IWSDK/WebXR delivery path.
- Two-foot seated interaction radius.
- Synthetic, provider-neutral fixtures only.
- Consequential actions remain proposals requiring explicit human approval.
- Neutral presentation with no recognizable third-party branding.
