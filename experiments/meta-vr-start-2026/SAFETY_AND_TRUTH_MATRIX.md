# Safety and Truth Matrix

The matrix distinguishes organizer requirements from Nymrel product and safety decisions. It is not a statement that eligibility or compliance has been established.

| Topic | Organizer requirement | Nymrel decision | Stage 0 enforcement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Adult/majority, eligible jurisdiction, Meta developer access, VR Start representative membership, no prohibited conflict | Make no identity or eligibility inference | Block registration and submission |
| Legal acceptance | Participation binds entrant to official rules and platform terms | Human review and explicit acceptance required | Record URLs and risks only |
| Hands-first use | Complete experience without paired controllers | Gaze, pinch, grab, bounded scroll, deliberate confirm/cancel | Document testable interaction contracts |
| Device support | Experience runs on Meta VR devices | Prefer IWSDK/WebXR if authorized | No install, build, or device claim |
| Seated use | Optimize for stationary two-foot radius | All primary panels within reach | Define radius evaluation |
| Judge access | Playable build remains free and available during judging | No deployment until account and legal gates clear | Keep packet local to source control |
| Submission video | Public YouTube/Vimeo video under three minutes | Show one complete inspect-to-decision loop | Plan only; do not record or upload |
| Originality | Entrant-built core; thin wrappers discouraged | Reusable spatial operator surface with deterministic local value | Preserve architecture independent of third-party service |
| Pre-existing work | Adapted entries need a meaningful competition-window feature | Treat Swarm Studio conservatively as adapted | Isolate new spatial delta and preserve provenance |
| Protected actions | Not prescribed by organizer | Consequential actions remain proposals | `execution=false`; no provider adapter |
| Data | Comply with Meta policies | Synthetic provider-neutral fixtures only | Reject secrets, private paths, and production endpoints |
| Branding | Avoid prohibited content; overview warns against recognizable third-party advertising | Neutral judge-facing view | No third-party logos or commercial advertising |
| Evidence | Judged on actual experience and presentation | Separate desktop, simulator, emulator, and hardware claims | Label exact runtime for every result |
| Cost | No purchase necessary | Spend exactly $0 without new authorization | No billing, credits, paid service, or wallet action |

## Claim controls

Permitted before implementation:

- “proposed,” “planned,” “documentation-only,” and “not yet verified”;
- references to official requirements with source and verification date;
- description of synthetic fixtures and local architecture.

Forbidden before evidence exists:

- “eligible,” “registered,” “VR Start member,” or “Meta-approved”;
- “runs on Quest,” “hands-first verified,” “deployed,” or “submission-ready”;
- “secure,” “immutable,” “production,” “live,” or “real-time” where the repository provides only fixtures;
- any claim that a local digest proves external provenance;
- any representation that human approval triggered a real protected action.

## Fail-closed conditions

Work must stop before implementation or publication if any of these remain unresolved:

1. controlling rules or required terms changed;
2. Meta developer access or VR Start membership cannot be verified;
3. entrant identity or representative authority is unknown;
4. selected dependency lacks acceptable provenance or license;
5. the build path requires payment, billing setup, a wallet, KYC, or an unavailable credential;
6. a required factual attestation is not known;
7. the division or pre-existing-code position would be misleading;
8. the proposed experience depends on private or customer data;
9. device evidence would be overstated;
10. deployment would create a public endpoint without explicit authorization.

## Receipt rule

Every material future change should record:

- exact commit SHA;
- changed paths;
- verification commands and results;
- dependency versions and licenses;
- evidence runtime;
- external systems contacted;
- whether credentials, money, publication, deployment, registration, or legal acceptance occurred.

Silence is not evidence. An unavailable capability must be stated as unavailable.
